Refunds & Returns Policy

At Proactive Research we take pride in supplying quality research materials and making every order smooth, secure and transparent.

Because our products are research grade compounds, we can't accept returns or give refunds once an order has been shipped or opened. This keeps the integrity, sterility and traceability of every item we supply intact.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, message us within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • Your name and order reference
  • Photos of the product and the packaging
  • A short description of what's wrong

We'll look into it promptly and arrange a replacement or a credit where it applies.

What can't be returned

For safety and compliance reasons we can't accept returns of:

  • Opened or used products
  • Products that haven't been stored as advised
  • Items ordered in error, or a change of mind

Refunds

Approved refunds go back the same way you paid, within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.

Need help?

Quality, compliance and your trust come first for us. If you have any questions or concerns about your order, message us and we'll help.
